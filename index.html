About Karachi Brasserie

An Elegant Escape in the Heart of Historic Saddar

Nestled inside Hotel Excelsior, directly opposite the Atrium Mall, Karachi Brasserie redefines the city's Continental dining experience. Designed with warm cream travertine stone pillars, ambient amber glass lighting, and distinctive chevron timber dining tables, the brasserie delivers a calm, polished atmosphere away from the bustling streets.

Whether you are joining us for an artisan double espresso, sharing a stone-baked Brasserie Special pizza, or indulging in our prime cut steaks and signature Parmesan Crusted Chicken, every plate is prepared with culinary passion, integrity, and warmth.

  • Prime Cuts & Fresh Seafood
  • Italian Stone-Baked Crusts
  • Handcrafted Espresso & Gelatos
